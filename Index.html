How a move works

Four steps from old keys to new

  1. Tell us about the move

    Send the quote form or WhatsApp us: where from, where to, the date and the size of the house.

  2. Get your quote

    We confirm the crew, truck size and packing needed, and agree a price before move day.

  3. Pack, load, drive

    Our crew arrives on time, wraps and boxes everything, loads carefully and transports it securely.

  4. Unpack and settle

    We offload room by room, reassemble what we took apart and leave you to enjoy the new place.

Questions
Before you book

      

        
How is the price worked out?
It depends on how much you are moving, the distance, the floors and access at both ends, and whether you want packing, unpacking or storage. Send the form and we will give you a clear quote before move day.

        
Do you supply boxes and packing materials?
Yes. When you book packing, our crew brings the boxes, wrapping and padding needed for your items.

        
Can you move us on a weekend or after hours?
Tell us your preferred date and time in the form. Office moves in particular are often planned for evenings or weekends to keep downtime low.

        
What if the new house is not ready?
We offer secure storage for short or long periods, and deliver your items when you are ready to settle in.
